# Rules and compliance digest

Last reviewed: **2026-08-11 (Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh)**.

Authoritative source: [Conditions of Participation and Challenge Rules](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1y_U1fJpahNUthKYFg1o3E2n99lO1tm7cyjf7_NrGJSo/edit?tab=t.0)

This file is an operational summary, not a replacement for the official English
rules. Recheck the source and all contest announcements before and during the
challenge because the organizer may change the rules, schedule, or contest period.

## Operational rules

- This is an **individual** contest. Do not collaborate or discuss the problem,
  ideas, generators, or code with another human contestant or any person other
  than personnel designated by the Challenge Director.
- AI tools are permitted. The official clarification shown to the participant
  states: “The use of AI tools, including generative AI and AI-assisted coding
  tools, is permitted during the Challenge.” The participant remains responsible
  for correctness, originality, licensing, and compliance.
- Internet research must be limited to **publicly available information**. Do not
  use information that requires authentication, including private cloud storage,
  private repositories, private groups, or another person's account.
- Use one legitimate participant identity/account. Keep the Codeforces and ICPC
  registration data true, current, and consistent; winner recognition requires a
  valid ICPC username/email entered during registration.
- Provide all compute resources locally or through lawful physical/cloud systems.
- Do not attack, overload, tamper with, exploit, or undermine the contest, judge,
  website, or other participants. An exploitational, fraudulent, harmful,
  misleading, invalid, or bad-faith submission can be disqualified.
- Follow the exact solution specification. A syntactically accepted submission is
  not necessarily high-scoring; submission and judging rates may be limited.
- Clarifications are the only official route for reporting an ambiguity or error.
  A correction does not automatically extend time or alter scoring.
- The problem-set scoring/ranking details and the Challenge Director's official
  clock govern. Dates and times may be changed, shortened, or extended uniformly.

## Submission ownership and licensing

- The submission must be the participant's original, good-faith work and must not
  infringe copyright, patent, trademark, trade-secret, privacy, export-control, or
  other rights.
- Pre-developed or properly licensed technology is expressly allowed by Challenge
  Rule 12, provided the participant owns it or has binding permission to use it and
  to grant the required contest license. This repository is intended as such
  pre-developed generic infrastructure.
- The Conditions also describe submissions as original to the Challenge and not
  substantively developed beforehand. Because this wording overlaps awkwardly with
  the express pre-developed-technology permission, keep pre-contest work generic,
  dated, and documented; do not claim that an unreleased problem-specific solution
  existed before the contest.
- Track every third-party component and its exact license. Be ready to provide a
  complete list of all open-source software in the submission if requested.
- Do not paste third-party code into the solution without verifying its license and
  documenting provenance. Public availability alone is not a license.
- The submission must contain no malware or prohibited/illegal content.
- Submissions are non-confidential. The participant grants the Organizer a
  perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use,
  store, reproduce, display, perform, and create derivative works from submissions
  for challenge administration and growth of competitive programming.
- Submitted entries may not be cancellable, deleted, amended, or returned. Treat
  every upload as final and preserve the exact submitted source locally.

## Challenge material and confidentiality

- Challenge data, algorithms, models, code, questions, downloads, and other
  non-public information disclosed by the Organizer/Sponsor are described as
  confidential. Do not publish or send them to other people.
- AI use is explicitly permitted, but the participant remains responsible for
  handling confidential material consistently with the rules and the chosen AI
  provider's data policy. Use only the approved contest workflow and avoid sharing
  material outside it.
- Public contest pages and public attachments may be researched, but never use
  leaked, access-controlled, or participant-only material obtained from others.

## Eligibility, completion, and prizes

- The participant must be of legal majority in their jurisdiction unless the
  organizer/sponsor agrees otherwise with suitable guardian consent, must be free
  of conflicting legal/contractual restrictions, and must create a Codeforces
  account and associate it with an ICPC account if requested.
- Sponsor/Organizer employees and officers, judges, organizers, promotion entities,
  involved agencies, specified immediate family, and household members are
  ineligible as detailed in the official Conditions.
- Review employer policies, laws, tax obligations, IP commitments, eligibility,
  and prize restrictions. The Organizer may request proof and has broad discretion
  to disqualify.
- The participant must attend and complete the entire challenge to be eligible for
  recognition or prizes.
- Winner selection is based on the highest combined score, subject to eligibility,
  verification, honest participation, feasibility, and compliance. Judge and DoCJ
  decisions are final. There is at most one prize per person per problem.
- Prizes are subject to availability, non-transferable, and may require a separate
  Prize Acceptance Form and personal-data disclosure. Winners pay applicable taxes
  and charges. Payment/delivery restrictions can affect some regions.
- Returned, unclaimed, undeliverable, forfeited, or unawarded prizes need not be
  re-awarded.

## Personal data and legal terms

- Participation authorizes collection and sharing of specified personal data by
  ICPC and Huawei for administration, prize fulfillment, communication, and the
  stated recruitment/hiring purposes. The official document lists retention and
  data-subject rights plus contacts at `manager@icpc.global` and `dpo@huawei.com`.
- The Conditions contain broad releases, indemnities, liability limitations, and
  network/cancellation disclaimers.
- Texas law governs the Conditions; specified disputes go to confidential
  arbitration in McLennan County, Texas under AAA Commercial Rules.
- If communications conflict, the official English Challenge Rules govern challenge
  issues. The organizer may publish revised rules without prior notice.

## Pre-submission compliance gate

Before every upload, confirm all of the following:

- The output format and all hard constraints pass the exact local validator.
- The source was produced by the participant with permitted AI assistance only;
  no human collaboration or non-public authenticated research was used.
- Every dependency/snippet has known provenance and a compatible license.
- No secrets, credentials, private data, debug output, absolute paths, or malware
  are present.
- Runtime and memory have safety margin on worst-case/adversarial instances.
- The submitted file is archived with hash, compiler mode, parameters, local score,
  public score, timestamp, and notes.
